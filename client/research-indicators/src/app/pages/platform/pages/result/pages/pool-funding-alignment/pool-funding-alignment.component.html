Why align bilateral results with Pool Funding?

Bilateral projects contribute significantly to CGIAR goals. This module enables you to link your result with Science Programs (SPs) or Accelerators and map its contribution into the Theory of Change (ToC) framework (Outputs and Outcomes). Once approved, this alignment can be synchronized directly with PRMS.

End-to-End Alignment & PRMS Sync Flow

From result creation to institutional PRMS reporting
1
Science Programs

Select the contributing Science Program(s) and designate one Primary SP.

2
Theory of Change

Map the Primary SP's Theory of Change (Outputs / Outcomes) and choose target indicators.

3
Review & Approve

Submit the result in STAR for validation and approval by the Principal Investigator or Center Admin.

4
PRMS Sync

Use PRMS SYNC in the sidebar to transmit the approved result and ToC mapping to PRMS via API.

Primary vs. Contributing Science Programs

The Primary SP is the main Science Program whose Theory of Change (ToC) targets are mapped. Contributing SPs represent secondary institutional collaborations.

Read-Only & PRMS-Sourced Locks

Results originating in PRMS or already synchronized become read-only in STAR to protect data integrity and prevent unintended discrepancies across platforms.
