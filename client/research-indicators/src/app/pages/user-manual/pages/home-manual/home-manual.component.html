Home

Home is the first screen you see after signing in to STAR. It gathers the announcements from the STAR team, shortcuts to the places you use most, the results you touched most recently, and a summary of everything you have reported so far.

1What this page is for

Home answers one question: what should I do next in STAR?

It does that with four things, all of them about you:

  • Any message the STAR team is currently publishing — deadlines, maintenance, new features.
  • Shortcuts to your projects, your results and your dashboard.
  • The three results you edited most recently, each with how complete it is.
  • A count of all your results, grouped by stage and by indicator.
person

Everything on Home is personal. Every figure counts only the results you created. A result reported by a colleague on the same project will not appear here, even when you can open and read it elsewhere in STAR.

2Getting to Home

Signing in takes you there automatically.

  1. Open STAR in your browser.
  2. On the sign-in screen, choose Continue with your CGIAR account. STAR does not ask you for a separate password — it uses your organisation account.
  3. Once you are in, STAR opens Home for you.

From anywhere else in STAR you can come back by clicking Home in the blue bar at the top, or by clicking the STAR logo on its left.

translate

The STAR interface is in English. Throughout this manual, button and section names are written exactly as they appear on screen, like this: Create Result.

shield

Cannot sign in? That is an account question rather than a STAR one. Contact Alliance IT Support at alliance-itsupport@cgiar.org.

5Welcome banner

Area 1 — the blue strip across the top.

It carries the STAR logo, a title and a short message. The STAR team writes that message and changes it whenever there is something you need to know: a reporting deadline, a planned maintenance window, or a feature that has just been released.

  • The text changes on its own — there is nothing for you to set.
  • The banner is informative only: it has no buttons and no links.
  • If the STAR team has not published anything, the strip appears without a message. That is normal.
lightbulb

Read it every time you sign in during reporting season. Deadlines are announced here first.

6Main actions

Area 2 — "Effortlessly access your key actions and get started."

Three large cards, each one a shortcut. Click anywhere on a card to follow it.

CardWhere it takes youUse it when…
My projects The Projects page, already narrowed to the projects you take part in. You want to look at a project or contract and the results attached to it.
My results The Results Center, already narrowed to the results you created. You want to search, filter, review or export your results.
My dashboard The Results Dashboard. You want the charts and totals rather than a list.
filter_alt

My projects and My results open with the scope set to My. Once you are there you can widen it to see everything you are allowed to see — the shortcut only chooses your starting point.

keyboard

Not using a mouse? Press Tab to move between the cards and Enter to open the one that is highlighted.

7My latest results

Area 3 — "View and track your most recent results."

Exactly three cards, showing the results you created that were changed most recently, newest first. It is a shortcut back to work in progress, not a list of everything you have.

  1. Indicator — which indicator the result belongs to, with its icon.
  2. Title — long titles are cut after 30 words and end with "…". The full title is on the result itself.
  3. Project — the contract code and name the result is attached to. It is missing when the result has no contract yet.
  4. Status — a coloured label with the current stage. Hover it to read what that stage means.
  5. Progress bar — how much of the result is filled in. It uses the colour of the status.
  6. Last update — when the result last changed.

Opening a result

Click anywhere on the card. STAR opens that result so you can keep editing or just read it.

The progress bar

Each result is made of several sections, and each section counts as complete once its required information is saved. The bar shows how far along you are and reaches 100% when the result is complete. Which sections are required depends on the indicator, so two results at "50%" are not necessarily missing the same amount of work.

checklist

To see exactly what is missing, open the result: its left-hand menu marks every completed section with a green check.

Creating a result

  1. Click Create Result. There is one in the blue top bar, always available, and a second one beside this section once you have at least one result.
  2. Fill in the few details STAR asks for in the window that opens.
  3. Confirm. The new result opens so you can start completing it, and it becomes the first card in this section.

8My results by status

Area 4 — the upper panel on the right.

One row per stage, and each row carries three things:

  • The name of the stage, for example Editing or Submitted.
  • A coloured bar. The longest bar is your biggest group; the others are drawn relative to it. It is a visual comparison, not a percentage.
  • The number of results at that stage.

Rows are ordered from the largest group down, and a stage with no results is not listed at all.

ads_click

Every row is a link. Click one and STAR opens your results already filtered to that stage — the quickest way to answer "which ones exactly?".

event

Before a deadline, click the Editing row: that is your to-do list.

9My results by indicator

Area 5 — the lower panel on the right.

A tile for every indicator STAR collects, each showing how many results you have reported under it. Indicators where you have nothing still appear, showing 0 — so you can see at a glance what you have not reported on.

  • Click a tile to open your results filtered to that indicator.
  • If the list is long, the panel scrolls on its own without moving the rest of the page.
help

Not sure which indicator your work belongs to? Open About Indicators in the Resources menu on the left for a description of each one.

12Frequently asked questions

The questions people ask most about this page.

Why can I not see a result a colleague reported?

Home only counts results you created yourself. To find other people's results, open My results and widen the scope, or use the Search box in the top bar.

I only see three results. Where are the rest?

That section is deliberately limited to your three most recently updated results. Your full list is in My results.

A result I just edited is not at the top.

The order follows the moment each result was last changed. Reload the page to pick up the newest information.

The progress bar is not at 100% but I think I filled everything in.

A section counts only once all of its required fields are saved. Open the result and look at the left-hand menu: the sections without a green check are the ones still missing something.

A status is missing from "My results by status".

Stages where you have no results are not listed. The stage will appear the moment one of your results reaches it.

An indicator shows 0. Is something broken?

No. Every indicator is listed whether or not you have reported under it, so a 0 simply means you have nothing there yet.

The banner announces something that is no longer relevant.

The STAR team publishes that message. Let them know through your usual support channel and they will update it.

The numbers do not match what I just did.

Home reads your figures when the page opens. Reload the page — F5, or ⌘ + R on a Mac — to refresh them.

13Glossary

Words this page uses that are specific to STAR.

STARThe Alliance Bioversity–CIAT platform for reporting research indicators.
ResultSomething your research produced or achieved that is worth reporting — a training course, an innovation, a policy change, and so on.
IndicatorThe category a result belongs to. It decides which information STAR asks you for.
StatusThe stage a result has reached, from being written through to being approved.
Contract / ProjectThe funded piece of work a result is attached to, identified by a code such as A1234.
Green checkThe tick shown next to a section of a result once its required information is complete. Green checks are what fill the progress bar.
Results CenterThe page listing every result you are allowed to see, with search, filters and export.
