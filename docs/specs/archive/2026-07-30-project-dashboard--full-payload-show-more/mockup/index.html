GATE-2 — "Show more" layout contract: DD-14, measured against two rejected mechanisms

Chunk A · project-dashboard/full-payload-show-more · mirrors the real grid and card markup from project-dashboard.component.html:153-201 and project-dashboard-card.component.html:52-79. Row counts for partners (137) and contributors (15) match the measured GATE-1 worst case exactly (design.md §13.1); this mockup's contacts (12) and levers (6) lists are illustrative and smaller than that worst case (measured at 35 contacts, 8 levers) — the geometry mechanism does not depend on the exact count, only on whether a section exceeds the 5-row collapse threshold, which all four do here. Mode 3 (DD-14) is what shipped and is the default here. Modes 1 and 2 are the two mechanisms that were tried and measured wrong — they exist so the human check has a control: if the defect they model does not appear, the check is not exercising anything real.

Six-step human check — requirements.md §7 DC-8, quoted verbatim

  1. Expand each of the four ranked cards in turn.
  2. Confirm the expanded card itself does not change height — its list scrolls inside the area the card already occupied. Under DD-14 this is the root check: if the card's height is unchanged, steps 3 and 4 follow by construction, and if it is not, they cannot be fixed downstream.
  3. Confirm its row-mate card in the nested 2-column grid does not change height and shows no empty gap below its own content — the row-mate's "Show more" must stay adjacent to its list, not float below a void.
  4. Confirm the right-hand column (Results by indicator, Results by status) does not stretch. Watch Results by status specifically — it carries flex-1 while Results by indicator is shrink-0, so any outward growth lands entirely inside that one bordered white card as a visible void. This is the step that failed under DD-13 and the reason the T-06 pivot happened.
  5. Confirm the page does not grow — no new vertical scroll on the page itself.
  6. Collapse everything and confirm the four cards are equal height again, exactly as before the change.

What this mockup settles

QuestionAnswer it gives
OQ-3 — bounded height: fixed, viewport-relative, or something else? Card-area-relative (DD-14) — neither. Mode 1 has no bound at all. Mode 2 is a fixed pixel bound (max-height:280px) and still fails, measured below. Only mode 3 removes the expanded render from intrinsic sizing entirely, which is why it is the only one that reaches zero in both directions.
DC-8 — which sibling does an unbounded expansion actually stretch? Both, and the second one is the trap. Select 1 · Unbounded and expand Results Partners: its row-mate in the nested 2-column grid stretches, and the whole right-hand column (Results by indicator / by status) stretches through the outer items-stretch — the page grows too.
Is bounding the list enough? No — and neither is a single static bound. This is the mode-2 lesson, and it is the actual T-06 pivot, not a hypothetical. A max-height clamps a box's max-content contribution only when the content exceeds it. Collapsed, 5 rows sit below the bound and contribute their natural height; expanded, the box contributes exactly the bound. The cap binds in one state only, so the difference still propagates through all four links — measured below, smaller than mode 1 but not zero. align-items:start (DD-13, the first attempt to contain the propagation rather than the growth) does not fix this either: alignment governs how a shorter item sits inside an already-sized track, never how the track is sized — so it cannot zero a growth it never touches. Mode 3 removes the growth at its source instead: the in-flow render is capped at 5 rows in both states, and the full list lives in an absolutely-positioned overlay that contributes nothing to the ranked grid's intrinsic sizing.
DD-4 — the colour consequence Collapsed, row 5 is #345b8f (middle), not navy. Expanded, row 137 is the one painted #112F5C. Encoding is computed over the full list in every mode here, so nothing already on screen changes when you expand — that is R-PDB-004, and you can verify it by eye in any of the three modes.
R-PDB-002 AC.2 — no toggle when ≤ 5 Contributing projects has 15 rows and Primary Levers 6, so both show a toggle; a section of ≤ 5 renders none. canExpand() lives in the card beside the toggle.
NFR-PDB-003 — a11y Each toggle is a real <button> with aria-expanded and an accessible name carrying its chart title, so four "Show more" buttons are distinguishable. Tab to them and press Enter/Space.
Render cost at the measured worst case 137 rows in one card, from GATE-1. Comfortable — the concern this mockup was originally going to test ("hundreds of rows") is not what the data contains.
Geometry — measured, not argued See the table below. Modes 1 and 2 are controls that must reproduce a real, positive delta or the probe is not measuring anything; mode 3 must read zero on every link, in both directions (no growth and no shrink), collapsed → expanded and expanded → collapsed-again, for one card at a time and for all four expanded together.

Measured 2026-07-30, chrome-headless-shell at 1440×1400 (window). Modes 1 and 2 are controls and both read non-zero, as required — mode 2's numbers land on the exact +52 / +13 figures design.md §6.3.2 and execution.md's T-06 record report for the real component, which is what makes this mockup's geometry trustworthy rather than merely plausible. Mode 3 reads zero on every link, in both directions (collapsed → expanded and expanded → collapsed-again), for each card individually and for all four expanded together — full raw JSON (this mockup's own ?probe=1 run, 22 measurement rows across all three modes) kept in ../evidence/mockup-dd14-measurements.json, alongside (not merged with) the component-level probes. Additionally verified: the DD-14 overlay genuinely scrolls rather than clips (scrollHeight > clientHeight, e.g. partners 5903>228, contacts 652>267), and the aria-hidden spacer contains zero focusable nodes in every mode — an aria-hidden subtree with a tab stop would itself be a WCAG violation (T-09).

Fidelity limits, stated so this is not over-read: system fonts stand in for Barlow / Space Grotesk (no external resources), the geographic card is a placeholder because Chunk A does not touch it, and this card's padding:18px is not the shipped p-5 (20px). The DD-14 spacer/overlay geometry rules — flex basis, min-height/min-width, visibility, position/inset/overflow — are transcribed from the shipped component (project-dashboard-card.component.html:52-62), and grid columns, gaps, bar geometry, rank chips, colour ramp, and toggle placement inside the items arm mirror it too. This is not class-for-class parity: three real DOM levels are deliberately collapsed (grid cell div.flex.flex-1 → card host block h-full w-full → section.h-full), and the rows-stacked-lever layout's sl-wrap div is omitted. None of these simplifications is geometry-bearing for what the probe below measures.
